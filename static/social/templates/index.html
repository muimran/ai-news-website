The rules, on one page

Everything here comes from the website: its colours, its three typefaces and its notch. Nothing new is added. Where the brief gives an approximate value, we use the website's exact one.

1 · Colour

Muted and Hairline are the website's own mixes: 60% and 14% Ink on Card. The brief's ≈#8E8D8A measures 3.2:1 on Card, below AA for small text; the website's #72716F passes.

  • Fraunces: line height 1.04 (quotes 1.10), spacing −0.022em. Its optical size is set to the size the reader sees, about 0.36 × the pixel size, so 144 px uses opsz 52.
  • Plex Mono: capitals, +0.07em. Labels and credits are the only words under 32 px, on purpose: capitals in a monospace read larger than they measure.
  • Bangla: headlines in Noto Serif Bengali 600 at 82% of the English size, line height 1.34. Labels and the mono's roles in Anek Bangla, no capitals, +0.01em.
  • The link-preview sizes are set here and get their first real test with template 15.

  • Built as on the website, at twice its size: the card's top-left corner is cut away so the grey shows through; the tab has Ink lines on its right and bottom only; the number box starts where the words start.
  • A thin strip of grey sits above the card, so the notch can be the website's size and still keep its words 60 px from the edge. On stories the strip is the 250 px that must stay clear anyway.
  • The notch names the type (Data, Quote, Explainer, Investigation, Correction), so the date sits beside it on its own. On a NEW post the notch says New and the type moves into the label line: Explainer · 17 Nov 2026.
  • Slides 2 to 5 of a carousel have no notch: the counter 02 / 06 sits in its place.

4 · Images

  • One treatment: duotone, shadows Ink #161615, highlights Background #E7E5E0, the same in light and dark. Greyscale first, levels stretched, then mapped. In Canva: Edit photo, Effects, Duotone, custom colours. In Photoshop: a gradient map with those two stops.
  • Every stock or illustrative image carries the credit Illustrative image · [Source], 16 px under it, right-aligned. On a full-bleed photo the credit sits on the sheet.
  • Screenshots stay as captured, because they are evidence: cropped to the part that matters, a 3 px Ink line round them, credited Screenshot · [Source], [Date].
  • Never AI-made images, robots, circuits or glowing brains.

  • Feed margins are 72 px on every side: past the 60 px safe zone, and after Instagram's 3:4 crop takes 34 px from each side, words still keep 38 px.
  • 36 px between text elements, 60 px under the notch row, 16 px between a picture and its credit.

BTemplates 1–6The carousel kit

The carousel kit, in light and dark

The six slides tell one sample story from start to finish, so they can be read in order. Every slide is 1080 × 1350 and works on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn. Variants change only position and size; fonts, colours, margins and the logo's place stay the same.

Template 1 · 1080 × 1350

Cover

Use it the first slide of every carousel: the one line that makes people swipe.

Short headlines (up to 6 words) are 144 px and take up to 3 lines; long ones (up to 14 words) are 96 px and take up to 5. A photo is a 360 px band, edge to edge, on the side the words are not. The arrow at the foot is the swipe cue.

Template 2 · 1080 × 1350

Text body

Use it one step of the argument, in up to 40 words.

A 96 px heading and 40 px text, about 40 words in all. The counter takes the notch’s place.

Template 3 · 1080 × 1350

Image body

Use it when a photo or a screenshot shows the point better than words.

Split: the picture is a 540 px band, or a screenshot inside the margins with a 3 px Ink line, and never touches the words. Full-bleed: the photo fills the slide and the words sit on a solid Card sheet, never on the photo; the logo or the counter gets a Card plate in its corner.

Template 4 · 1080 × 1350

Data

Use it when one number, one comparison or one run of dates carries the story.

The number is 360 px with its unit at 96. In charts the bar that makes the point is Ink and the rest are Muted, with values written on the bars. The source always sits at the foot.

Template 5 · 1080 × 1350

Quote

Use it when a source says it better than we can.

The quote is 80 px. Under it, the speaker’s name and role, or a description for a source we don’t name; the source line says where it comes from.

Template 6 · 1080 × 1350

Closing

Use it the last slide: send readers to the full story, or ask one question.

The notch is optional here. Use it to close the carousel the way it opened; leave it off for a question.

CBlanksTo fill in

The same six, with placeholders

The slots a newsroom fills in: [NOTCH LABEL], [LABEL], [HEADLINE], [TEXT], [IMAGE], [NUMBER], [QUOTE], [SPEAKER, ROLE], [SOURCE] and [LOGO]. [TEXT] is the one addition, for body copy and captions, which the list had no name for.

DIn the grid3:4

The covers survive Instagram's crop

Instagram's profile grid shows only the middle 3:4 of a 4:5 post, losing 34 px from each side. The notch and the headline start 72 px in, so both survive whole, light or dark. The grey strip and the notch make a carousel recognisable in the grid before anyone reads it.
